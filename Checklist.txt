Do's:

1- Use respectful language in all posts and comments

2- Share academic achievements and projects

3- Keep profile pictures and bios professional

4- Credit sources when sharing content

5- Adjust privacy settings to control visibility

Don’ts:

1- Post offensive or discriminatory content

2- Share confidential academic or personal information

3- Engage in online arguments or trolling

4- Use slang or emojis in professional posts

5- Tag professors or employers in casual content
